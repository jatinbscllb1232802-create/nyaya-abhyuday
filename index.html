About the Fest

Where Law Meets Technology

The Moot Court Committee at the School of Law, Forensic Justice and Policy Studies, National Forensic Sciences University, Gandhinagar proudly presents Nyaya Abhyuday – The Techno-Legal Fest.

Nyaya Abhyuday is a dynamic platform designed to engage aspiring legal professionals with the rapidly evolving interface of law, technology, and justice. Built on academic excellence and experiential learning, the fest bridges the gap between classroom knowledge and real-world legal practice.

Last edition saw enthusiastic participation from more than 55 teams representing top National Law Universities and leading law colleges across India. Nyaya Abhyuday returns with greater ambition to elevate this legacy further.

Flagship Events

Two Competitions. One Vision.

Technological Moot

4th NFSU National Technological Moot Court Competition

Explore cutting-edge legal issues emerging from technological advancement. Argue complex questions of AI accountability, autonomous weapon systems, constitutional rights, and state responsibility in the digital age.

  • Appellate-style advocacy
  • Memorials from both sides (Bluebook 21st)
  • Preliminary → Quarter → Semi → Final
  • Researcher Test (counts for Prelims ranking)
  • Focus: AI, defence tech, constitutional safeguards
Trial Advocacy

2nd NFSU National Trial Advocacy Competition

Immerse yourself in a realistic courtroom simulation. Master examination-in-chief, cross-examination, objections, evidence handling, opening statements and closing arguments — the essential tools of effective litigation practice.

  • Full trial simulation with witnesses
  • Witness examination & court craft focus
  • Practical courtroom strategy
  • Objections, evidence marking, etiquette
  • Persuasive advocacy under pressure
The Dual Advantage

Why Compete in Both?

Most competitions test one skill set. Nyaya Abhyuday deliberately puts teams through both the appellate courtroom of the future and the classic trial court — so you leave as a more complete advocate.

Technology Moot

Builds deep research, constitutional analysis, and appellate advocacy on emerging tech-law questions (AI liability, autonomous systems, accountability frameworks).

Trial Advocacy

Builds pure courtroom craft — examination, cross, objections, evidence handling, and the ability to think on your feet in a live trial setting.

Combined Ranking

Performance across both tracks shapes the overall standing. Teams that master research + practical advocacy stand out to judges and future chambers.

Rules Overview

Competition Format

Team Composition

3 members: 2 Speakers + 1 Researcher

Language

English only

Mode

Physical · NFSU Gandhinagar

Memorials

Both sides · Max 2500 words · Bluebook 21st Edition

Oral Timings

Prelims 25 min/team · Quarters & Semis 45 min · Final 60 min

Overall Scoring

Memorial 50 + Oral 50 · Researcher Test feeds into Prelims

Assessment Snapshot

Written Memorial (50)

  • Knowledge of facts & law — 10
  • Articulate analysis — 10
  • Extent & use of research — 10
  • Format & citation — 10
  • Grammar & style — 10

Oral Rounds (50 per speaker)

  • Marshalling of facts — 5
  • Articulation of issues — 5
  • Presentation & communication — 5
  • Response to court questions — 5
  • Application of legal principles — 15
  • Use of authorities — 10
  • Courtroom etiquette — 5
